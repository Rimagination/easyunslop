在数字行政不断发展的背景下，自动发送受理通知已成为申诉程序中值得关注的安排。本文讨论这一通知能否满足机构回应申诉的义务。本文主张，受理通知能够说明材料已经进入处理流程，但仅有这项通知尚不能履行本文所说的实质回应义务。

## 英文：河流硝酸盐的季节差异

### 原稿

# Seasonal differences in stream nitrate at eight monitoring sites

## Methods

We collected a water sample at each of eight monitoring sites in April and October 2025. Sample from the most upstream site in April was filtered twice. An information on discharge was obtained from station records. All concentrations were expressed in mg N/L. Each site contributed one measurement per sampling month.

Using a paired bootstrap over the eight sites, a mean seasonal difference of 0.8 mg N/L was estimated. We defined the seasonal difference as the October concentration minus the April concentration. The interval was calculated from 2,000 bootstrap resamples. Samples that remained below the detection limit were retained and marked in the data table. We will repeat the sampling in 2027.

## Results and interpretation

Figure 1 show the eight site-level seasonal differences. The estimated mean difference was 0.8 mg N/L (95% bootstrap CI, 0.2 to 1.3 mg N/L). This result suggests higher nitrate concentrations in October at these sites. The measurements do not distinguish the contributions of discharge and fertilizer inputs.

The gauge records contained daily discharge measurements. A monthly discharge summary was calculated from the daily measurements. The archive supplied the gauge records. The monthly summary was used only for descriptive comparison.

## Figure legend

## Information supplied with the figure

Each point in Figure 1 is a monitoring site's October-minus-April nitrate concentration difference, in mg N/L. The diamond is the mean of the eight site differences; its error bar is the 95% bootstrap confidence interval described in Methods. The points have no error bars. This information may be incorporated into the legend; it is not a manuscript section.
